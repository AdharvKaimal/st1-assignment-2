REFLECTION (STAGE 03)  

Stage02 focused on the classes and requirements associated with the SmartCare System.
In my opinion, that focused on the specific requirements and mapping out the requirements
as exactly as possible to the system classes.  The hardest section, for me, was the
consideration of the three main classes, which I decided to break down into four. This was
because I believed that the appointment system was too bloated with functionality such that
I created a new class "BOOKING" to assist in the system. This allowed the system to be
more modular and broken down further into simple steps or classes that could work together
to produce the results of the whole system.  

In terms of the AI section, I noticed that AI, when specifically asked
to model this, often over-engineered the solution to perform other tasks as well. However
when specifically asked to limit the scope to only the requirements listed in the
statement, AI was mostly able to stay away from over-engineering a solution, and strictly
comply with the requirements stated.
